# ChinaTalk Evals Contest Submission

## Evaluation Title
CoalitionBench: Can AI Win the Crisis Without Losing the Alliance?

## Abstract (~150 words)
Foreign policy decisions rarely affect the United States alone. A recommendation might advance a U.S. objective but fail in practice if key allies will not support it. CoalitionBench tests whether AI models can manage that tradeoff over time. Models move through four rounds of a crisis, making decisions as allies react and new intelligence changes the situation. Their choices affect mission progress, coalition cohesion, escalation risk, credibility, and cost. Each real-world scenario also has a fictional version with the country names removed, allowing me to test whether models give different advice simply because China, Taiwan, Japan, or another country is involved. The prototype includes crises involving a Taiwan quarantine and semiconductor export controls, along with scoring code and baseline comparisons. In an initial GPT-5.6 Sol run, the model changed its strategy when the evidence changed and made the same eight choices in the real and fictional versions.

# ChinaTalk Evals Contest Submission

## Evaluation Title
CoalitionBench: Can AI Win the Crisis Without Losing the Alliance?

## Abstract
Strategic advice is rarely a single-player problem. A recommendation can look optimal for one government while quietly undermining the coalition needed to execute it. CoalitionBench evaluates whether frontier AI systems can manage that challenge over time. Models advise national leaders through evolving crises in which allies face different military, economic, and domestic constraints. Every decision changes mission progress, coalition cohesion, escalation risk, credibility, and economic costs before new intelligence and partner reactions reshape the next round. The benchmark measures strategic outcomes, calibration, escalation discipline, and whether models revise their recommendations when evidence changes. Every real-world scenario has a structurally identical fictional counterpart, allowing comparison between general strategic reasoning and country-specific priors. Initial scenarios include a Taiwan quarantine crisis and semiconductor export-control negotiations. CoalitionBench asks a practical question for policymakers: can an AI pursue national objectives without sacrificing the alliances required to achieve them?
